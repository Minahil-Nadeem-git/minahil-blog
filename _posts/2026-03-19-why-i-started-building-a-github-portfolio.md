# 🚀 Why I Started Building a GitHub Portfolio

## 🎯 A Small Idea That Changed My Perspective

As a Computer Engineering student at UET Lahore, Faisalabad Campus, I used to think university life was mostly about attending lectures, submitting assignments, and preparing for exams. Most of my work stayed on my laptop, and once a semester ended, many of those files were forgotten.

That changed when one of our instructors suggested that we should create a GitHub portfolio to document our learning journey. The idea sounded simple, but it changed the way I looked at my work.

Instead of treating assignments and projects as temporary tasks, I started seeing them as part of my growth as an engineer.

---

## 💻 Why GitHub?

Before starting this portfolio, I thought GitHub was only for professional developers and large software teams. Over time, I realized that it is also one of the best places for students to document progress, practice consistency, and build confidence.

This repository helps me:

* record what I learn throughout my degree
* share projects and experiments publicly
* reflect on challenges and lessons learned
* build a digital footprint as a Computer Engineering student
* track my growth over time

Years from now, I want to look back at these posts and clearly see how far I have come.

---

## 📖 More Than Just Code

Many people think GitHub is only for uploading source code.

For me, it serves a bigger purpose.

This portfolio is becoming a personal learning journal where I can write about:

* academic experiences
* semester challenges
* technical projects
* lessons learned from mistakes
* time-management struggles
* personal growth as an engineering student

Not every lesson comes from a successful project. Some of the most valuable lessons come from missed deadlines, difficult weeks, and unexpected challenges.

Those experiences deserve to be documented too.

---

## 🌱 Building a Digital Footprint

One of the biggest reasons I started this portfolio is to create a digital footprint of my university journey.

Every semester introduces new concepts, new technologies, and new challenges. Without documentation, many of those experiences are eventually forgotten.

By writing these posts, I can preserve important moments from my academic life and create a timeline of my development as an engineer.

Rather than only showing final results, I want this portfolio to reflect the complete journey—including the mistakes, struggles, and lessons that helped me improve.

---

## 🔍 What You Can Expect Here

Over the coming semesters, this portfolio will include posts about:

* Programming Fundamentals
* Database Systems
* engineering projects
* problem-solving experiences
* study techniques
* academic challenges
* personal reflections

My goal is not to present a perfect story.

My goal is to present an honest one.
