# ⚡ The Week Everything Became Hectic

## 🎓 When Everything Arrived at Once

For most of the semester, things felt manageable.

There were lectures, labs, assignments, and quizzes, but nothing seemed impossible to handle.

Then suddenly, everything changed.

Around two weeks before finals, almost every course had deadlines approaching at the same time.

Assignments were due.

Projects needed attention.

Quizzes were being announced.

Lab work was still pending.

And final exam preparation had not even started.

What had once been a normal semester quickly turned into one of the busiest periods of my university life.

---

## 📚 The Assignment Marathon

One thing I remember clearly is how common it became to work on multiple assignments in a single day.

It was not unusual for my classmates and me to be working on:

* One assignment in the morning
* Another assignment in the afternoon
* A quiz preparation session at night

Sometimes we were switching between completely different subjects within a few hours.

One moment I would be focusing on a technical task, and the next moment I would be preparing for an upcoming quiz.

The workload felt endless.

---

## 😵 Trying to Keep Up

The biggest challenge was not a single assignment or quiz.

The challenge was handling everything together.

Every task seemed urgent.

Every deadline felt important.

Every course required attention.

Many days felt like a race against time.

Instead of following a structured schedule, I was simply moving from one deadline to another, trying to complete whatever needed to be submitted next.

---

## 🤝 Everyone Was Going Through It

Interestingly, I was not the only one feeling overwhelmed.

Almost every student around me was dealing with the same situation.

Classrooms, labs, and group chats were filled with conversations about deadlines, submissions, quizzes, and exam preparation.

Although stressful, it also reminded me that university is a shared experience.

Everyone struggles at some point.

Everyone faces difficult weeks.

And everyone learns how to push through them.

---

## 💭 Looking Back

At the time, those weeks felt exhausting.

However, looking back now, they taught me something important:

> University is not only about learning technical concepts. It is also about learning how to perform under pressure.

The deadlines eventually passed.

The quizzes ended.

The semester moved forward.

But the experience remained.

Even today, whenever I think about the busiest week of my semester, those final weeks immediately come to mind.
